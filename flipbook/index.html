Park Classification · Research Question

The Comprehensive Plan defines Resource-Based Parks as parks managed primarily to preserve significant environmental resources. Are there Local Parks that better fit that definition?

296

22

182

Methodology
  • Policy review: Comprehensive Plan definitions became evaluation criteria.
  • GIS overlay: park boundaries intersected with the Resource Protection Area (RPA) layer to compute RPA share.
  • Benchmark: non-zero Resource-Based Parks have a median RPA share of 72.88% (IQR 45.09–87.27%), the scale used to grade Local Parks.
  • Qualitative review: development intensity, facilities and function; two screening methods compared.
  • Stream valleys: 182 parcels without RPA checked for drainage, sewer, trail and SWM easements.
Franconia Forest · 6.7 ac · 72.92% RPA. Undeveloped and wooded: a strong candidate.
Eakin–Mantua · 72.2 ac · 94% RPA. Active fields and courts, so RPA alone does not justify reclassification.

Study Area · Shenzhen Talent Park
Algorithm
Model

Objective = 0.8 × completion time + 0.2 × cost. Trucks travel Manhattan distances; drones fly straight lines with a 5 km range and payload limits.

Solution

MILP in Gurobi with nearest-neighbor TSP warm starts; a genetic algorithm improves routes. Optimality gaps of 1.59–1.87%.
